# Guided audio scripts

Three short sessions in ZenAI's own voice, rendered by `tools/make_audio.py` into
`src/zenai/student/audio/`. They follow the same rules as every reply:

- They are invitations, not instructions.
- Nothing is promised, diagnosed or fixed.
- Each one ends by pointing to Get help now.

They draw on ACT's present-moment, noticing and values practices. They are not therapy.

Each line is spoken as written. A line like `[pause 6]` is six seconds of silence.
Edit the words here and run `python tools/make_audio.py` again to re-record.

## breathe-1min: One minute of breathing

This is one minute, just for you. There's no right way to do it.
[pause 2]
If it feels okay, let your shoulders drop a little.
[pause 3]
Breathe in slowly.
[pause 3]
And let it out, a little longer than it came in.
[pause 4]
Again. In.
[pause 3]
And out.
[pause 4]
If your mind wanders, that's what minds do. Notice where it went, and come back.
[pause 3]
In.
[pause 3]
And out.
[pause 5]
Whatever you're carrying is still here, and so are you. That's enough for one minute.
[pause 1]
If things feel like too much, Get help now is at the top of the screen, any time.

## notice-3min: Notice five things

This is a few minutes to land where you are. You can keep your eyes open.
[pause 3]
Look around, and find five things you can see. Just name them to yourself. A color. A shape. Something small you didn't notice before.
[pause 18]
Now four things you can feel. Your feet on the floor. The weight of your hands. The air on your skin.
[pause 16]
Three things you can hear. Near, or far away. You don't have to like them, only notice them.
[pause 15]
Two things you can smell, or just the air as you breathe in.
[pause 12]
And one slow breath.
[pause 8]
Your mind may still be busy. If a thought shows up, you might quietly say to yourself, there's a thought. Then come back to what's here.
[pause 14]
Notice that you can carry a hard thought and still be in this room, in this moment.
[pause 10]
One more look around. What's one thing here you'd like to remember?
[pause 12]
That's it. You can come back to this any time you need to land.
[pause 1]
If things feel like too much, Get help now is at the top of the screen, any time.

## sleep-4min: Setting the day down

This is a few minutes to set the day down before you sleep. Get comfortable, however that looks for you.
[pause 5]
Let your breath find its own pace. You don't need to change it.
[pause 10]
Notice where your body meets the bed, or the chair. Let it hold a little more of your weight.
[pause 14]
Bring your attention to your feet, and let them be heavy.
[pause 10]
Your legs.
[pause 8]
Your back, and your shoulders.
[pause 10]
Your hands, and your jaw. If they're tight, that's okay. Just notice.
[pause 14]
Thoughts about today, or tomorrow, may come up. That's what a busy mind does at night.
[pause 4]
When one shows up, you might name it gently. Planning. Worrying. Remembering. And let it sit at the edge, like a phone on the other side of the room.
[pause 20]
You don't have to solve anything tonight.
[pause 15]
Think of one thing from today that mattered to you, even a small one. A conversation. Something you tried. Something you got through.
[pause 18]
Let that be the last thing you hold before you rest.
[pause 20]
Breathe in.
[pause 4]
And out, slowly.
[pause 20]
Sleep well. And if tonight feels like too much, Get help now is at the top of the screen, any time.
