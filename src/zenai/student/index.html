Privacy and data

Everything is off until you turn it on. It stays on this device unless you turn on sync, and nothing you write is ever shared with the university.

Your journal and values answers
What you write in Journal, and in "What matters to you", stays on this device only. ZenAI never reads it and never sends it anywhere. Delete a journal entry any time with one tap, or erase everything below.

What you type is sent to ZenAI to write a reply. Names, emails, phone numbers and student IDs are removed before anything reads it. Conversations aren't saved on this device unless "Keep this conversation" is on. If you talk out loud, your browser's speech service (Apple or Google) turns your voice into text; ZenAI only ever receives the text and never keeps audio. Your journal and values answers are never sent to ZenAI at all.
